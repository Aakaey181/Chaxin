# Market Research Skill

Use this skill for competitor, user, pricing, and source ecosystem research.

## Workflow

1. Read `AGENTS.md`.
2. Read `agents/market-researcher.md`.
3. Define research question and decision it supports.
4. Gather only traceable evidence.
5. Separate facts, hypotheses, and open questions.
6. Produce a compact recommendation with confidence level.

## Output Format

- Research question
- Evidence table
- Competitor or source matrix
- User pain points
- Pricing or positioning hypothesis
- Confidence level
- Follow-up research

## Guardrails

- Do not cite unsourced claims.
- Do not treat platform access as safe without source onboarding and compliance review.
